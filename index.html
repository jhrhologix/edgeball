Wall treats the image as a side view: gravity pulls down, shelves and table tops hold the ball. Floor treats it as a table seen from above at an angle: the ball shrinks and slows as it moves toward the top (farther away). With the live camera, move your hand or objects in front of the lens and the walls move with them. Tap Freeze to lock a level.
